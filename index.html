🏡 Before he arrives

Moka is very food motivated and will go looking for snacks, so a quick sweep of your space helps:

  • Keep food off low tables and counter edges, and make sure the trash is covered or out of reach.
  • Put away any grapes or raisins (see Health for why).
  • Pick a quiet spot for his crate and bed where he can still see or hear people. He likes being near you.

🦴 Feeding

  • Moka gets 1 cup of kibble per day, split across at least two meals: ideally one in the morning and one in the afternoon or evening.
  • His purple scoop holds ½ cup, so one scoop per meal is perfect.
  • You can use his orange Bobble toy or his regular food bowl. Either works great.
  • You can use his orange Bobble toy or the bowl from his bag.
  • He doesn't have a set mealtime (unless you use the autofeeder), so timing is flexible. Several smaller meals are fine too, as long as it's at least two meals and 1 cup total.
  • He doesn't have a set mealtime, so timing is flexible. Several smaller meals are fine too, as long as it's at least two meals and 1 cup total.

💧 Water

Tap or filtered water is fine, he has no preference. Just keep a bowl out and he'll drink as he needs. There's a bowl in his bag, but feel free to use your own.

🐕 Walks

  • Moka gets two walks a day: one in the morning and one in the afternoon or evening. Go whenever works for you. He can hold it for up to 10 hours (sometimes more), so no need to stress if life gets busy.
  • If you can't do a full walk, it's totally fine to let him into our dog park for a few minutes to do his business.
  • Harness only, please. It's easier on his back (see Back care).
  • His leash has a locking mechanism. Make sure it's set before you head out.
  • Keep him on leash whenever he's outside. The only exception is a fully enclosed dog park, and please leash him up again before any gate or door opens. He's a herding dog and will chase after small animals, so we worry about him running into traffic.
  • He's in a new place, so be extra careful at your door. Leash him up before opening it so he doesn't slip out.
  • Treats from the orange bag (or a zip-lock of his kibble) are great for keeping his attention.
  • He can be stubborn and may lunge at food on the ground. Feel free to redirect him firmly or offer a treat. No judgment!
  • Please avoid other dogs for now. He's usually great with them, but scuffles do happen once in a while. If a dog approaches and Moka starts leaning away or showing signs he's uncomfortable (stiff body, ears back, turning his head away, lip licking), gently pull him back and put some distance between them. He might snap. He has never bitten another dog, but the snapping can be scary.
  • In the fall there's often trash or dead animals on the ground. If he lunges at something or tries to roll on it, it's probably something gross, so pull him away.
  • Extra poop bags are in the orange bag.

🌙 Nighttime

Before bed, Moka usually gets a quick potty break and a Greenie (the green bones in the bag) as a nighttime snack.

This is optional. Skip it if he's been out recently or you'd rather not. As long as he gets his two walks, he'll be fine.

Sleeping: Moka loves to sleep with people, but he's just as happy on his own bed or in his crate if you'd prefer. He's very comfortable with any of these, so pick whatever works best for you. If your bed is tall, though, it's best to have him sleep on his own bed so he isn't jumping down from it (see Back care).

🚪 When you're away

When you head out, Moka can hang out in our office or his crate. He's comfortable in both.

Office: It has his dog beds, toys and a water bowl. Toss a few treats into his autofeeder bowl or drop them in the water bowl (a nice way to get him drinking), then say "stay". Refill the water if it gets low.

Crate: Spread a little peanut butter or put some treats in one of his black Kong toys (there should be two), then say "Crate" and he should walk right in. Leave the Kong in with him and close the door. He may bark at first, but that's just FOMO. He'll settle.

When you head out, his crate is the best spot. Dogs are denning animals, and Moka is very comfortable in his crate, especially somewhere new.

If you'd rather leave him loose in a room, pick one with his bed and a water bowl and nothing he can get into. Dropping a few treats in the water bowl is a nice way to get him drinking.

🐾 Back care

Moka had a mild scare with IVDD (intervertebral disc disease), which affects the discs in the spine. He's doing well right now, but we want to protect his back.

  • Please limit jumping down from higher places as much as you can. Tall beds count too.
  • Going up and down the couch or using ramps is totally fine.
  • Walk him with his harness only, not a collar.

🚨 Health & emergency

Contact us or a vet if you notice any of these:

  • Lethargy or unusual unresponsiveness
  • Blue or pale gums
  • Nonstop vomiting or diarrhea

Moka occasionally has vomiting spells, which isn't necessarily alarming. There's a probiotic and an acid controller in the orange bag that may help. Please check with us before giving either and we'll walk you through it.

If he gets into something he shouldn't, you can usually just watch him for the signs above. Dogs are surprisingly resilient!

⚠️ The one exception is grapes or raisins. They can cause kidney failure very quickly. If Moka eats a grape or raisin, call us immediately or take him straight to the vet.

❓ FAQ

Moka ran away!

Don't chase him. Grab a Greenie (or another treat, or some kibble) and call for him. Say "GREENIE!" He knows it's a high-value treat and will come back.

He's walking weird, with his back hunched up.

This could be an IVDD flare-up, so contact us ASAP. If you can't reach us, keep him in his crate with low activity (only potty breaks and meals) until you do. If it looks bad, contact a vet.

He won't stop barking.

He may be overstimulated. Try a walk to tire him out, or put him in his crate to calm down. If the barking is making you uncomfortable, please reach out. We want you to feel comfortable as his sitter!

🚪 外出时

你出门时，Moka 可以待在我们的书房或他的狗笼里。两个地方他都很自在。

书房：里面有他的狗床、玩具和水碗。往他的自动喂食碗里撒几块零食，或者丢进水碗里（这样能鼓励他多喝水），然后说 "stay"（别动）。水不多了记得加。

狗笼：在他的黑色 Kong 玩具（应该有两个）里抹一点花生酱或放些零食，然后说 "Crate"（进笼），他就会自己走进去。把 Kong 留在里面陪他，然后关上门。他一开始可能会叫，但那只是怕错过热闹，很快就会安静下来。

你出门时，他的狗笼是最好的地方。狗天生喜欢待在小窝里，Moka 在自己的狗笼里很自在，在新环境里更是如此。

在他的黑色 Kong 玩具（有两个）里抹一点花生酱或放些零食，然后说 "Crate"（进笼），他就会自己走进去。把 Kong 留在里面陪他，然后关上门。他一开始可能会叫，但那只是怕错过热闹，很快就会安静下来。

🐾 背部护理

Moka 之前有过一次轻微的 IVDD（椎间盘疾病）虚惊，这种病会影响脊椎里的椎间盘。他现在状况很好，但我们想好好保护他的背。

  • 请尽量减少他从高处往下跳。高的床也算。
  • 上下沙发或者走坡道完全没问题。
  • 遛他时只用胸背带，不要用项圈。

🚨 健康与紧急情况

如果发现以下情况，请联系我们或兽医：

  • 精神萎靡或反应异常迟钝
  • 牙龈发青或发白
  • 不停地呕吐或腹泻

Moka 偶尔会有一阵呕吐，这不一定值得担心。橙色包里有益生菌和抑酸药，可能会有帮助。给他吃之前请先问我们，我们会告诉你怎么做。

如果他吃了不该吃的东西，通常只需要观察有没有上面这些症状就好。狗狗的恢复力其实很强！

⚠️ 唯一的例外是葡萄和葡萄干。它们会很快导致肾衰竭。如果 Moka 吃了葡萄或葡萄干，请立刻打电话给我们，或者马上带他去看兽医。

❓ 常见问题

Moka 跑掉了！

不要追他。拿一根 Greenie（或者别的零食、狗粮）叫他回来。大声喊 "GREENIE!"，他知道那是超级好吃的零食，会自己跑回来。

他走路怪怪的，背是弓起来的。

这可能是 IVDD 发作，请尽快联系我们。如果联系不上，在联系上之前让他待在狗笼里，尽量少活动（只出去上厕所和吃饭）。如果看起来很严重，请联系兽医。

他一直叫个不停。

他可能是太兴奋了。可以带他出去遛一遛消耗体力，或者让他进狗笼冷静一下。如果他的叫声让你觉得不舒服，请联系我们。我们希望你照顾他的时候也能感到自在！
